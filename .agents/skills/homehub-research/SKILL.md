# HomeHub Research

Research is read-only unless the user asks for a durable registry or issue update.
`docs/PROJECT_SPEC.md` remains accepted product truth; current issue contracts own
bounded tracked work.

For broad scans, focused idea probes, or hardware-opportunity research, read
`references/research-workflows.md`.

When editing `docs/Future_Development.md`, also read `references/registry.md`.
For generative/LLM/multimodal proposals, read `docs/AI_SEMANTIC_LAYER.md` before
recommending provider calls, tool access, or automation authority.

Use fresh primary evidence for current technology, standards, products, APIs, or
feasibility. Prefer existing hardware/data before new purchases. Separate facts
from hypotheses and do not promote research into accepted product policy.

Report the strongest useful findings, existing owner/dedupe status, evidence and
uncertainty, and the smallest experiment or observation that would resolve it.
